Research

Hybrid zone of white-bearded manakins (Manacus manacus)

Doctoral thesis project, Colombia

Male manakins gather at leks, cleared display courts where they perform for females. Where populations with different plumage meet, those displays and the genes behind them can be tested against each other in nature.

My thesis combines field observation with genomic data to describe how this zone is structured and what keeps the lineages distinct, or lets them blend.
